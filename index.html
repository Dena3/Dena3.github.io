My Favorite Topic: Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

I am really fascinated by Artificial Intelligence and how machines can be trained to think and learn like humans. Machine Learning is a branch of AI that allows computers to improve themselves through experience, without being explicitly programmed. Some of the most exciting applications include neural networks, natural language processing, and computer vision. These technologies are already changing the world through things like self-driving cars, medical diagnosis, and AI assistants. You can learn more at IBM's AI Resource Center.
